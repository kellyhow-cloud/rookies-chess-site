Common questions

I reinstalled, or got a new device. How do I get Rookies again?

Rookies is a paid app. Redownload it from the App Store while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it, at no extra charge. Your progress follows you through iCloud.

Is there a subscription or in app purchase?

No. Rookies is a single one time purchase with everything included: every lesson, puzzle, piece set, and the full game review. No subscriptions, no in app purchases. Family Sharing is supported.

Does my progress sync between devices?

Yes, through your own iCloud, as long as you are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud enabled.

Does Rookies collect my data?

No. Rookies collects no personal data, shows no ads, and uses no trackers. See the Privacy Policy.

What devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad running a recent version of iOS and iPadOS.

I found a bug or have an idea.

Please email us with your device model and iOS version, plus a short description. Screenshots help.
