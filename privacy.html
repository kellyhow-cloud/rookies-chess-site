Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026
Rookies collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no third party trackers. Your game progress stays on your device and in your own iCloud.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers. We do not operate servers that receive your data.

Where your data lives

  • Your progress, settings, and earned items are saved on your device.
  • If you are signed in to iCloud, this information may sync across your devices through Apple's iCloud key value storage. That data is controlled by your Apple account and covered by Apple's privacy policy. We cannot see it.

Purchases

Rookies is a paid app. Your purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store, so we never receive or store your payment information. There are no in app purchases and no subscriptions, and the app supports Family Sharing.

Children's privacy

Rookies is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect data from children. There are no social features, messaging, or external links to unknown content inside gameplay.

Third parties

Rookies includes no third party advertising, analytics, or tracking software.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
